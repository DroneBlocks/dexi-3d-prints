# DEXI 10 Guards

Prop guard parts for the DEXI 10.

| File | Part |
|---|---|
| `DEXI 10 Guard Top.step` | Top guard piece |
| `DEXI 10 Guard Bottom.step` | Bottom guard piece |
| `DEXI 10 Guard Coupler.step` | Connects the guard pieces at each motor arm |
| `DEXI 10 AVR Guard Adapter.step` | Lets existing AVR guards be fitted to the DEXI 10 |
