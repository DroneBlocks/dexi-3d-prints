DEXI 5

DEXI GPS Mount Assembly and Cable Routing

Holds the dexi-gps board at the back of the DEXI 5. The cable runs inside the Tower.

Parts

Print in PLA. Tower standing up, GPS Holder flat side down, Lid top side down.

DEXI GPS Mount TowerBolts to the frame.
DEXI GPS Mount GPS HolderHolds the board. Twists onto the Tower.
DEXI GPS Mount LidTwist-on cover.

Hardware

QtyPartUsed for

1

Thread the cable through the Tower

Push the flight-controller end of the GPS cable down through the top of the Tower. It comes out under the base.

2

Bolt the Tower to the frame

Remove the two rear frame screws. Put the Tower lugs over the holes and screw them back in with M3x12 screws. Keep the blue washers on top. Check the screws are still tight after the first few flights.

3

Route the cable

Run the cable down through the rear opening in the frame and plug it into the GPS port on the flight controller.

4

Fit the GPS board

Press the 4 nylon nuts into the bottom of the GPS Holder. Push the cable up through the middle hole and plug it into the board. Screw the board on with the arrow pointing to the front.

5

Twist the GPS Holder onto the Tower

Set it on turned a little clockwise. Press down and turn it anticlockwise until it clicks.

6

Twist on the Lid

Put the Lid on and turn it clockwise until the arrow on top points to the front.

To remove

Push the small tab under the GPS Holder up, turn the GPS Holder clockwise and lift it off.

Cable: use a straight-through 6-pin GH cable, about 300 mm long.
